WHOLESALE FAQ

Clear answers for professional buyers.

A concise guide to the purchasing process, prepared around the information buyers usually need before requesting a quotation.

What is the minimum order quantity?

The minimum order quantity is 50 PCS. Final assortment and commercial terms are confirmed in the quotation.

Can I request a quotation for my market?

Yes. Share your quantity, destination market and sourcing requirements through the wholesale enquiry form for a tailored quotation.

Can requirements be adjusted?

Specific colours, construction, packaging and private-label requirements can be discussed according to quantity and production feasibility.

How are production timelines confirmed?

The production schedule is agreed after specifications, quantities, pricing and payment terms have been confirmed.

How is dispatch arranged?

Dispatch arrangements, freight responsibility and destination-related requirements are agreed before shipment.
